A CHURCH FOR THE COMMUNITY

Faith that brings us together.

St George's is a Christ-centred Anglican church committed to loving God, loving people, and serving our neighbourhood. Whether you have followed Jesus for years or are simply curious, there is a place for you here.

We gather around Scripture, prayer, worship and genuine friendship — creating space for people of every age and stage of life.
